# 14: How the Orb, Floating Orb and Caption show Hands-free and the cues

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 12

## Question

Hands-free is an on/off separate from the four Jarvis States (see **Domain: Utterance, Listening, Push-to-talk and a waiting Jarvis State**). So the Orb and Floating Orb show two things at once: the Jarvis State, and whether the mic is open. How should they look and sound?

- The distinct "mic open" look (from **Privacy**), worn in every State, including during a Push-to-talk hold with Hands-free off.
- Idle with Hands-free on (waiting quietly) against Idle with it off.
- A look while Speaking that shows a tap only cuts the Reply short (from **Interrupting**).
- The "heard you, queued" cue for speech during Thinking, which stays Thinking.
- How the Caption and Transcript mark an Interrupted Reply.
- Three sounds that must be told apart: Hands-free on/off, the acknowledgement when a Turn is sent (from **Does this spec also shorten Thinking?**), and the queued-speech cue.
